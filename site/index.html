How it works

Four steps. No amounts leaked.

The plugin never holds keys or signs. Every action is a proposal the user approves in their PayBox wallet.

  1. 01

    Deposit

    Public USDC goes in and becomes an encrypted Private USDC balance. Only the deposit amount is public.

    private_usdc_deposit
  2. 02

    Send privately

    The amount is encrypted, bound to the sender and the contract, then moved between encrypted balances. A send above balance moves 0 instead of failing in public.

    private_usdc_send
  3. 03

    See your balance

    Sign a short-lived Fhenix permit in PayBox. Fhenix re-encrypts your balance so only that permit can read it.

    private_usdc_balance
  4. 04

    Withdraw

    Unshield some or all of it, then claim real USDC with Fhenix's decryption proof. The claimed amount becomes public.

    private_usdc_withdraw · claim

Encryption takes longer than PayBox's 10-second limit per call. See the hard part below.

The hard part

14 seconds of math. A 10-second window.

Each PayBox call starts fresh, so every private send downloads Fhenix's public keys and builds a zero-knowledge proof from scratch.

Safety

Nobody can touch your funds. Including us.

No admin withdraw

The owner can change deposit caps and pause new deposits. Nothing else. A test fails if any other admin function appears.

Pause never traps funds

Pausing stops new deposits only. Sends, withdrawals and claims keep working.

Scoped, expiring permits

Viewing permits read only this contract's balance, cannot move funds, and expire after 30 minutes by default.
